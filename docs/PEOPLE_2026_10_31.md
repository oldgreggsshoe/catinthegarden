# Villagers and cart drivers — 31 October 2026

User request: "make person models with different clothes and hair and whatever
and walking animation and put the same people in their carts when they're
driving".

## What was added

- **`people.rs` / `people.wgsl`**: one cached "superset" low-poly body (unit
  height, feet at the origin, facing +y). Every garment, hair style, beard and
  hat is in the mesh and tagged with a garment group and the choices that show
  it; each instance's selection bits collapse the parts that person does not
  wear (whole triangles share a tag, so a hidden part degenerates). Choices:
  trousers or a bell skirt (stockings under it), none/long open coat/cloak,
  apron (matched to the garment under it), cropped/short/long/bun/ponytail/curls,
  no beard/full beard/moustache, no hat/flat cap/straw hat/hood/headscarf/tall felt
  hat, sleeved or rolled forearms. Colours: 8 skin tones, 9 hair colours (grey and
  white for older people), 15 natural dyes and undyed cloth, leather shoes;
  height 1.56–1.88 m, girth 0.9–1.23. `Person::from_key(key)` is a pure function.
- **Skeleton**: 11 rigid bones (pelvis, torso, head, upper arms, forearms,
  thighs, shins). Joint angles come from three per-instance numbers — walk phase,
  walk weight, sit weight — blended between standing (breathing, small sway),
  walking (opposed leg/arm swing, knee lift early in the swing, bob, counter-twist;
  shorter stride in a skirt) and sitting (thighs forward, shins down, hands on
  knees). Head turn is a fourth number. No CPU skinning.
- **Identity**: `people::resident_key(village_name, index)`, 24 residents per
  village. Traffic picks each cart's **driver** as an unused resident of the
  route's home village (`Route::home`, `Agent::driver`), or a traveller for
  through routes. The village crowd excludes residents currently driving. The
  same key gives the same look on the footpath and on the bench.
- **Walkers (`crowd.rs`)**: the village path worker now also returns each
  village's footpath network as a walk graph (`Walkway`: nodes, links, door
  nodes, the court mouth), built from the same desire-line trails it routes.
  For the nearest 8 villages (within 2.5 km), 2–9 residents each stand about,
  pick a destination (home, the court mouth, another door, anywhere), walk the
  shortest path at their own pace, keep 0.35 m left of the centre line (so
  people coming the other way pass), slow down behind someone going the same
  way, turn at a walking rate, and at a door often go indoors (fade out) for
  10–50 s. Heights come from the drawn terrain (`village_raster_height_at`,
  path shaping included) for those within 450 m. Scene time drives it, so F10
  pauses and the time-speed keys scale it (dt capped at 2 s per frame).
- **Drivers (`traffic_render::driver`)**: the baked driver box/sphere is gone
  from the NPC meshes. Each cart's driver is a person instance on the bench
  (hips on the 1.32 m seat for any height), head turning slowly. At a village
  stop (8–20 s dwell) a stop long enough (≥ ~8 s) is choreographed: across at
  seat height clear of the rail and wheel, down to the near side, round the
  back to face the engine jar, back, and up — continuous in position and facing
  (`Choreography`, pure function of elapsed/total dwell). Drivers in a bore are
  lit by the bore's lamps (same lamp lists as the carts).
- **Renderer**: one pipeline, near (<40 m, superset body 17,424 vertices) and far
  (3,546: coarser facets, no face details/curls) mesh ranges, a soft blended contact
  shadow under people on foot, up to 512 instances, 450 m draw distance with the
  traffic-style dithered fade. Drawn after villages; drivers are drawn even in
  enclosed tunnel views, walkers only when the outdoors is visible.

Controls/flags: `PLANET_VILLAGERS=0` empties the footpaths; `PLANET_DISABLE=people`
hides everyone (A/B timing).

## Not done / limits

- No children; adults only. No conversations, gestures, carrying things, or
  sitting on benches in the village. Walkers do not go into the parking court or
  onto harbour piers, and only the 8 nearest villages are populated.
- Walkers avoid each other only by keeping left and following; standing people
  can be walked through. They are not obstacles to the player.
- The player has no body of their own yet (the player's cart is still empty).
- Drivers sit with hands on knees (there is no horse or reins to hold).
- Climbing down still brushes the front wheel top for a moment.
