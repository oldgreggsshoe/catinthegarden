# Round 19 — eight chosen views, two viewpoints

Ian's call, after round 18's siting work showed no single viewpoint on this
planet holds ice and rock in every direction: **"now that we know this
limitation, it's fair to choose the best 8."** The reference photographs are
eight deliberate compositions, not a compass sweep, so this matches them.

## How the eight were chosen

Not by taste alone. At each of two viewpoints, 72 compositions were rendered --
24 azimuths by three pitches -- and every one scored on what a photograph of
this place contains: snow, rock, some sky, no farmland, and tonal spread. The
top candidates were then **looked at**, because the score cannot tell rock from
shaded snow and cannot see a seam line.

- **Views 1-4, the ridge at pz 991,1290**, standing 3.7km above ground at 74km.
  This is the site with genuine rock, and the one that supplies the mixed
  rock-and-snow faces.
- **Views 5-8, the original icefield camera.** Kept deliberately: framed level
  rather than 30 degrees down, it gives horizon, distant peaks and sky. Much of
  round 17's "no sky, no horizon, wall-to-wall white" was **the downward framing
  of the old scenario, not the site**, which is worth knowing on its own.

Rejected on sight: every composition with a visible diagonal seam line (the
ridge's 60-105 degree arc has them badly), and everything looking out over the
green and yellow lowland.

Frame 61.3ms, all assertions pass. View 4 still carries a faint seam at its left
edge -- the cleanest available at that bearing.
