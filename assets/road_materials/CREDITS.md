# Road timber material

`weathered_oak.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on
23 October 2026. Original output is copied unmodified (1254x1254 RGB). No
external texture pack or licence dependency. The renderer builds a cached
1024x1024 sRGB albedo / linear grain-height texture with 11 linear-light mips;

asset; the shader orients it along boards/posts and adds the joints separately.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic seamless tileable PBR BASE COLOR texture of natural weathered structural oak timber, square image. This is a raw material texture for a realistic 3D game, not a scene or photo of an object. Orthographic flat scan, uniform neutral diffuse illumination, NO lighting gradients, NO shadows or highlights. Continuous wood surface without board divisions, plank seams, edges, nails, inscriptions or borders: the engine adds those separately. Fine crisp organic oak grain runs consistently horizontally left-to-right, with irregular flowing growth lines, tiny pores and restrained naturally curving cathedrals. A few small subtle knots with smoothly flowing surrounding grain, not strong repeated dark knot spots. Mature warm medium brown and grey-tan oak with subtle weathering, NOT orange or golden varnish, NOT purple. Realistic restrained tonal range, rich fine millimetre grain visible close up, no blurry stretched noise bands, no exaggerated cracks, no black stripes. Dry matte unfinished timber, slightly worn and gently rough, not rotten. Seamless on all four edges. Texture fills entire image. Request 1024x1024 pixels.
