I added and ran an ignored baker throughput instrument using 200,000 deterministic candidate positions, 8 directions, and the current 5 ranges against an in-memory generated height field. Three optimized runs measured 1.49M, 1.51M, and 1.41M rays/s, averaging 1.47M rays/s (7.35M profile samples/s). At that rate, the exhaustive 100m global scan for the current 4,000km-radius planet would take about 30.4 hours. This measures the check loop, not terrain generation, erosion, export, or filesystem I/O.
