Fixed the buoyancy bug and pushed it.

Root cause: the surface camera was asking the rendered-surface sampler for ocean depth. That sampler intentionally resolves all ocean-floor heights to sea level, so depth was always zero and the wave amplitude/velocity were effectively disabled. The camera therefore remained at the static 0.255m equilibrium height.

Changes:
- Added a resident raw-bathymetry query for ocean depth.
- Buoyancy now receives real depth, wave height and wave vertical velocity.
- Added a rising/falling-wave regression proving the camera actually bobs.
- Land walking, gravity, slopes and jumping are unchanged.

Validation:
- 343 app tests passed.
- `cargo check --workspace` passed.
- Commit `b8c74b3` pushed to `origin/experiment/instant-global-forests`.

Pronunciation note: Americans generally say “buoyant” as **BOY-ant**, not “boo-ee-ant”. “Buoy” is usually **BOO-ee**, because English enjoys inconsistency.
