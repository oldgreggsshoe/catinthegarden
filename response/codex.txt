The screenshot/behaviour had two causes:

1. Near shallow coasts, ground contact was winning even though a water surface existed. A jump could therefore become `grounded` and use land gravity. Water surfaces now remain swimming mode; the seabed is not treated as a land landing while water is present, so buoyancy handles the return.
2. The renderer adds three camera-local vertical ripple waves (180m/70m/28m), but CPU collision only sampled the broad six-wave swell. The visual surface could therefore move up/down while the camera followed a different surface. CPU height and analytic velocity now include the same local ripples, with the same depth weighting.
