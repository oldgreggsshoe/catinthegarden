The fixed-water diagnostic was still being corrected only during movement. A streaming/terrain refresh could leave the camera at the previous wave radius, while the HUD had already updated to a new wave height. I now resolve the fixed water radius after streaming as well as during movement, and use the fixed 1.0 m eye margin from the sampled wave.

To remove the remaining coarse-triangle mismatch, fixed mode now disables the short 180/70/28 m ripple geometry (and horizontal Gerstner transport) in both CPU and WGSL. The broad vertical swell remains animated and is sampled identically by camera and mesh. Buoyancy mode is unchanged and can be restored with WATER_BOBBING_ENABLED = true.
