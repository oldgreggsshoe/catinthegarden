Clear-water mode: press K (or start with PLANET_CLEAR_WATER=1).
- Underwater fog is off, so visibility is infinite.
- The lamp has infinite range (L still turns it on).
- The water surface is about 50% transparent, above and below, so the ship and seabed show through. It is a pixel checkerboard (half the water pixels are skipped), not a smooth blend, because the ship is drawn after the water and a normal alpha blend would not show it. A smooth version needs a separate blended water pipeline; say if you want that.
