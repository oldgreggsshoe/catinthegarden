The dark horizontal band is not a new mountain feature. It is the ocean/lake shell leaking through the fixed-L7 flat-triangle terrain at a mixed-LOD boundary. The repeated vertical fins are coarse fallback/skirt triangles (HUD: 223 fallback chunks out of 229) being exposed while the ocean draw is allowed for source tiles whose L4 footprint contains both land and water. In flat mode the per-fragment ownership/height rejection is too coarse at this grazing view, so water appears over/through land. It is a rendering/LOD ownership bug, not the procedural bake itself.
