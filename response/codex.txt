Tuning:
- 125.0: spatial frequency. Lower = wider/fewer ranges; higher = narrower/more.
- 3: octave count. Lower to 2 to remove fine spiky substructure.
- 0.30: activation threshold. Higher = fewer peaks; lower = more coverage.
- 0.50: transition width. Larger = softer, broader slopes; smaller = sharper cutoff.
- 34_000.0: vertical amplitude. Lower to reduce height/spikiness.

A good first softer trial is frequency 64, 2 octaves, threshold 0.36, transition 0.64, amplitude 24,000. Then rebuild the baker, back up assets/outmaps/test-planet, run the production bake command, and restart the app so it reloads the outmap.
