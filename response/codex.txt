Implemented and pushed as `6b88719`.

**Shore fix:** screen-space refraction was returning zero transmission at viewport/bank discontinuities, so the ocean fell back to opaque blue and produced rectangular patches. It now samples the current pre-water sandy bed/depth pixel when the refracted ray misses, only accepting geometry behind the water surface, then applies the same 30m exponential attenuation. This keeps the shore continuous instead of blue blocks.

**Beach colour:** shared pale tropical cream-yellow sand (`sRGB 0.94, 0.89, 0.70`). The existing depth transmission naturally mixes it with blue: shallow water stays turquoise/sandy; increasing depth attenuates the bed and returns to blue.
