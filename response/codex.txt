Yep — that really is the literal Arctic Circle.

The bake classifies every surface above ±66° latitude as `Ice`, and flat-triangle mode displays the categorical biome directly with no material blending. That makes the biome boundary a perfect hard polar cap; it is not a terrain hole or lighting failure.

For the normal renderer it is softened by the snow/material blend. If we want the flat experiment to look less cartoonish, the next fix would be a latitude/temperature fade at the cap boundary while keeping the underlying biome data unchanged.
