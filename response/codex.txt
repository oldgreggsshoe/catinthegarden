Startup breakdown (28.5 s to frame 4 on this run; the earlier 34.7 s run varied by a few seconds):

Shader/pipeline compilation is the bulk:
  terrain: render pipelines            4.99 s   (ground planet)
  moon:   terrain: render pipelines    4.00 s   (the sky moon builds a whole second TerrainRenderer)
  foveated: direct pipeline            2.03 s
  foveated: remaining tail (warp/unwarp pipelines etc.)  4.81 s (phase "foveated renderer")
  => roughly 16 s of ~28 s is the driver compiling pipelines.

Data work:
  foveated: build and upload six faces  2.17 s  (CPU stitch 0.91 s, queue.write_texture 1.08 s, max mips 0.14 s)
  terrain: ocean FFT/foam/spray         0.72 s (+0.57 s for the moon's copy)
  terrain: cubemap + material textures  0.53 s (+0.81 s for the moon's copy)
  terrain: root tile reads              0.27 s (+0.29 s moon)

Ideas, biggest first:
 1. wgpu pipeline cache (disk) so compiled shaders are reused on the next launch.
 2. Don't build a full TerrainRenderer for the moon at startup (build it lazily or share pipelines/shader module): saves about 6 s.
 3. Build the foveated ray renderer lazily on first F5 (about 7-9 s with its pipelines); it is off by default.
