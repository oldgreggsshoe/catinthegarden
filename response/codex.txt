Yes — and I think real low-resolution Earth relief is the best next step.

**Best fit: NOAA ETOPO 2022, Ice Surface, 60 arc-second.** It is a seamless global land-topography **and ocean-bathymetry** grid, includes Greenland/Antarctic ice surfaces, and is available as GeoTIFF or NetCDF. Sixty arc-seconds is roughly 1.8 km on Earth, already finer than our 4096×2048 baker grid (~6.1 km per equatorial sample), so higher source resolution would currently be wasted. NOAA states the data are not subject to US copyright protection. [NOAA ETOPO dataset and downloads](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model), [official metadata/licensing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ngdc.mgg.dem%3Aetopo_2022)

Other valid choices:
- **GEBCO 2026:** newer global land/ocean relief at 15 arc-seconds (~450 m), explicitly public domain with attribution, but its full grid is about 7 GB and far beyond our present bake resolution. [GEBCO 2026](https://www.gebco.net/data-products-gridded-bathymetry-data/gebco2026-grid)
- **Copernicus GLO-90:** free worldwide 90 m land data, but it is a surface model including vegetation/buildings and does not provide useful ocean bathymetry. It is unnecessary overkill here. [Copernicus DEM](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM)

Your sharpness observation is right. The current broad terrain is softened twice: the macro working grid is coarse, and the procedural ridge fold deliberately uses softened creases. I would **not** sharpen that globally — it previously produced terrace-like edges. Instead I would:

1. Use ETOPO as the authoritative macro height/coastline, without smoothing its heights through our authored continent generator.
2. Recompute hydrology, climate and biomes around that real shape.
3. Derive a broad ruggedness/curvature mask from ETOPO.
4. Use that mask to sharpen only the long procedural ridge octaves in mountain belts, while plains and rounded hills stay soft.
5. Keep the current 3× positive-land presentation for the first comparison, with the existing bake backed up.

That should give us recognisable real ranges and sharper selected ridges, while procedural detail supplies everything below the source resolution. I recommend ETOPO 2022 60-arc-second as the import source.
