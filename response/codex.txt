The “largest octave” is the two 1,400 m swells (the first and second waves). The current fixed diagnostic already disables the 180/70/28 m local ripples, but the 160/65/24/9 m global waves are still active.

For a quick manual test, set the amplitudes of global waves 3–6 to zero in both places:

- `crates/app/src/ocean.rs`, `WAVES`: entries with wavelengths 160, 65, 24 and 9 m: change `amplitude_meters` to `0.0`.
- `crates/app/src/shared_planet.wgsl`, `ocean_surface`: change the amplitudes in the `third`, `fourth`, `fifth` and `sixth` `gerstner_wave` calls to `0.0`.

Leave the first two 1,400 m waves unchanged. Keep the CPU and WGSL edits paired or the camera collision height will diverge from the rendered water. The fixed diagnostic (`WATER_BOBBING_ENABLED = false`) will also keep the short local ripple layer off. For a cleaner long-term option, add a named `OCEAN_LARGE_SWELL_ONLY` toggle and branch both implementations rather than editing amplitudes manually.
