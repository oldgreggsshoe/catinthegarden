The hard edge is real and I can see it in the latest capture. It is not the sand colour or the wave phase.

Cause: the ocean is a separate analytic sea-level shell. At the first positive terrain sample, `fs_ocean_transmission` discards the shell (`terrain_height_hint > 0`), while the terrain pass starts rendering sand behind it. Because the shell writes opaque depth and the terrain is a separate mesh, their ownership boundary becomes a crisp silhouette. The 50m beach colour blend only changes terrain colour; it cannot soften that geometric edge.

A proper fix needs a shoreline transition in geometry/compositing: either conform the near-shore water shell to the terrain height, or make a narrow edge band use depth-tested translucent water before the dry-sand terrain. Simply widening the sand colour mask or removing the discard would just paint water over land and create a different artifact.
