- **Mountainous ocean:** The raster path interpolates mixed coastline triangles between sea-level ocean vertices and raised land vertices, then shades part of that slope as ocean. The best next step is a true L5 bake from an 8192x4096 working grid, but the dense ray-field allocation rises from roughly 0.286 GB to 1.142 GB; test 16-bit ray height packing first. Monotone bicubic sampling could only soften, not restore, missing detail.
