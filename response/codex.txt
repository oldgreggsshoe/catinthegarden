The latest screenshot exposed a second, separate bug. The camera was being pinned at the old `PLANET_CORE_CLEARANCE_METERS = +0.02m` sea-level floor. When a wave fell to about -28m, the camera stayed at +0.02m, so the HUD correctly calculated roughly 28m of clearance—but that was artificial clearance, not buoyant following.

I changed the safety floor to -100m (below the 53m maximum storm trough), and widened the LOD radius assertions to accept that bounded underwater position while still preventing entry into the solid planet. The camera can now descend with a trough instead of sticking at sea level. Underwater rendering remains deferred.
