25 September - F10 slowdown diagnosed and fixed (not yet confirmed live).
Cause: with CATINGARDEN_OCEAN_FFT=1, F10 starts time and the birds. Each bird samples the water at 6 different times, and the CPU FFT surface kept only one cached grid, so every sample re-ran a 2.3ms inverse FFT (~20s per frame).
Fix: 8-slot time-keyed grid cache in ocean_fft.rs, plus a regression test. Please press F10 again with FFT on and tell me the frame rate.
