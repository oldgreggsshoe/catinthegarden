Three sensible options:

1. **Minimal, flat-mode fix (recommended):** keep the baked biome data, but soften the ±66° edge over a few degrees. Each triangle still gets one final colour, so it remains flat-shaded; only the cap boundary stops being a perfect circle.
2. **Better Earth-like bake:** change the hard `|latitude| > 66° => Ice` rule to roughly 72–75°, while retaining snow/ice for genuinely high terrain. This needs a rebake but gives a much smaller, more Greenland/Antarctica-like cap.
3. **Most realistic:** derive ice from an authored/observed land-ice mask, with latitude and elevation only as fallbacks. More work and extra source data.

I’d do **1 first** for this visual experiment, then decide whether the bake itself should move to option 2. That avoids changing the planet data just to make the wireframe presentation less literal.
