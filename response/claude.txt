28 September - Settings for Ian's "sideways spikes" screenshots

Short answer: SWELL=30 is the best-supported setting. There is no sign of WIND 50 / CHOP 0 / WAVELENGTH 0.6. The logs don't record environment variables, so no screenshot's exact settings can be proved.

Which screenshot is which:
- "Sideways spikes" is manual/1790520906-735221 (27 Sep 15:55, commit 3e190ee). Its jagged sideways shards were caused by the slope-aware fold floor in 1142d30, which Claude reverted the same afternoon ("Needle-crest fix reverted" in AGENTS.md; "drawn_fold_census" in HANDOFF). They cannot be reproduced on the current code whatever the settings. Your broad pointed foam patches being "less extreme" is expected.
- Your ocean_swell_shards replay is built from manual/1790525174-746683 (commit 316d3ba), after the revert. That is the right screenshot to chase.

Settings evidence: the ship's wave height at t=0 (the first "ship float" record in each log.jsonl) is a fingerprint of the sea settings.
- 1790520906, 1790524927, 1790525069 (the "~30m swell" float log) and 1790525174 all read -18.7706, so they share one setting.
- That day's plain launches (1790518487, 1790520762) read -3.5403.

On a clean rebuild of 3e190ee, interactive launch, FFT=1:
- no overrides: -1.0498 (not -3.5403)
- SWELL=30: -18.0332 (closest)
- SWELL=30 WIND=50 CHOP=0 WAVELENGTH=0.6: -15.1509
- WIND=50: -1.7607; WIND=20: -0.1384; WIND=30: 0.4020
- PEAKS=0: -0.7969; CHOP=0: -0.9900; WAVELENGTH=0.6: -1.1379

Neither baseline matches a clean build, and no single WIND/CHOP/PEAKS/WAVELENGTH value restores -3.5403. So the binary Ian ran then differed from clean 3e190ee (probably uncommitted changes in the shared checkout at the time), and an exact fingerprint match is not possible.

Recommendation: keep PLANET_OCEAN_FFT=1 PLANET_OCEAN_FFT_SWELL=30 with no other overrides. Judge the replay against 1790525174-746683, not 1790520906-735221.
